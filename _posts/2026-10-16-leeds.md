*a dream, recorded on waking*

in a work environment  
toxic hazard  
nylon shirts and polyester suit  
why am i here — just looking  
hurried now  
everything on edge  
why? it's all long gone  
time moves colleagues are past tense  
an office in Leeds

*you should be somewhere else*

so drinks  
there is this place  
not far we'll cross the tracks  
on a tour bus to dilapidation  
but not Beeston Hill twat

*you're forgetting something*

on the corroded road  
wind whips litter  
i know i need to be somewhere else — where  
on the railway bridge  
graffiti 'crafted now'  
am i crafted

down there come on  
buildings nameless and faded  
we criss we cross  
there is no other way

*you're forgetting something*

but drinks — it's a cafe  
it's 'meats'  
they're already ahead  
keep up  
hot fetid cabbage boiling  
smell of stewing meat  
rows of tables old wooden chairs and benches  
plastic coverings

menu with pictures  
Nothing to eat just slabs of black and grey  
chops, beef, liver and kidney  
chicken skewer,  
nausea rising

*you should be somewhere else*

they went to the back — a squeeze  
some woman is in front  
boxed between a wall and table  
get the drinks  
no going back  
no getting through

now the bar — how  
i don't want to be here  
butterflies and churn  
noise, odor and body sweat  
their mouths move  
i've lost the words  
i need to be somewhere  
not here — wake up
