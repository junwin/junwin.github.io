<h1>About</h1>

I'm John Unwin. I build software and take photographs, and this site is
where those two things meet.

By day I design and build systems — resilient, scalable things that have
to keep working. Away from that, I photograph whatever catches my
attention: travel, architecture, flowers on a walk, the light on a
lakeshore.

My photos are on the [Gallery]({{ '/gallery/' | relative_url }}) page, and I share them at:

- **Mastodon:** [mastodon.social/@jdunwin](https://mastodon.social/@jdunwin)
- **Bluesky:** [junwin57.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/junwin57.bsky.social)
- **Pixelfed:** [pixelfed.social/junwin](https://pixelfed.social/junwin)
- **Tumblr:** [johndunwin](https://www.tumblr.com/blog/johndunwin)
- **GitHub:** [github.com/junwin](https://github.com/junwin)

Most of the photo pipeline here is automated: my images carry
machine-generated descriptions and alt text (from my
[image_description](https://github.com/junwin/image_description)
project), and the best-performing shots are promoted to this site's
gallery based on engagement scores.
