Data Deletion Instructions

John Unwin Media Facebook publishing app · Last updated 13 October 2026

John Unwin Media is a personal-use application for publishing content to Facebook Pages administered by its operator. It does not provide public user accounts.

Remove the app's Facebook access

  1. Open Facebook's Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps and Websites (the label and location may vary).
  3. Find johnunwinmedia and choose Remove.

This revokes the application's access through Facebook. Removing the application does not automatically remove posts already published to a Page; those can be managed directly on Facebook.

Request deletion of application-held information

Email jdunwin@gmail.com with the subject John Unwin Media data deletion request. Please describe the information you want deleted and provide enough detail to identify it. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

The operator will review the request, delete applicable information held by the application where possible, and respond to confirm the outcome. Data held independently by Meta is governed by Meta's own settings and policies.
