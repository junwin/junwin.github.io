Privacy Policy

John Unwin Media Facebook publishing app · Last updated 13 October 2026

John Unwin Media is a personal-use application operated by John Unwin to manage and publish content to Facebook Pages that he administers, including John Unwin Photo. The application is not offered as a public service, and visitors do not create accounts with it.

Information accessed

When authorized through Meta, the application may access a Facebook account identifier, the list of Pages the account manages, Page publishing permissions, and content needed to create or manage posts. Meta provides access tokens to authorize these requests.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate with Meta and publish or manage content on authorized Pages. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or selling personal information. The application does not intentionally collect data from visitors to this website.

Storage and sharing

Authorization tokens and any working copies of publishing data may be retained in the operator's development environment as needed for the application. Information is sent to Meta to perform authorized Facebook operations and is not sold to third parties. Meta handles information on its services according to its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Information used by this application is retained only as needed to operate or troubleshoot it. To request deletion of information held by the application, follow the data deletion instructions. You can also revoke the application's Facebook access through your Facebook settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact jdunwin@gmail.com.
